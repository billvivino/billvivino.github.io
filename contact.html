Pricing & Budget FAQ

Which pricing models are available?
Dedicated Implementation provides weekly development with pricing agreed after inquiry. Fixed-price builds are quoted after paid discovery. One-on-One Engineering reserves Bill’s personal attention during reserved engagement hours at an agreed rate, with recurring weekly capacity or longer engagements by agreement.

How much should I plan for?
Dedicated Implementation is quoted after we discuss your workstream. Custom builds begin with a separately agreed discovery fee, followed by a fixed build quote. Our Working Prototype runs 4–6 weeks from kickoff, with scope and price agreed upfront. Production releases are custom scoped, typically starting at $25,000.

How are pricing and payments agreed?
We confirm scope, availability, and payment terms before checkout. Dedicated Implementation pricing and billing frequency are agreed in writing before a private payment link is sent. Cancel before the next billing date to stop future charges. Fixed-price installments, if agreed, pay for a defined project. Paid discovery does not commit you to the subsequent build.

What does each commitment cover?
Weekly Dedicated Implementation covers one active workstream, engineering review, testing, and progress updates; it does not reserve a fixed number of labor hours. See the weekly delivery terms. A fixed-price build covers the accepted deliverables. One-on-One Engineering reserves exclusive, hands-on engagement hours, including live collaboration. Changes to scope or budget require agreement.
