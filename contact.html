Project Budget FAQ

How much should I plan for?
Prototype retainers generally fall in $5k–$10k, MVP retainers in $10k–$25k, and full-application work in $25k–$50k. Larger or multi-phase engagements may exceed $50k. These are planning ranges; the accepted proposal states the exact fee.

How are project retainers paid?
Choose the discounted prepaid total or the standard total through a fixed number of weekly payments. The first payment is due before work begins.

Does fixed price guarantee every outcome?
No. The fee covers the proposal-defined delivery phase and deliverables. Unknown conditions and work outside that scope are reviewed and approved separately.
