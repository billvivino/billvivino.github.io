Project Budget FAQ

What does a typical MVP cost?
Most MVP engagements fall between $15k–$40k, depending on scope and platform. Existing MVP completion and rescue work begins with the current state of the product and codebase.

How are larger engagements structured?
Larger implementation engagements generally use time-and-materials billing with a weekly cap. Scope can be paused or adjusted at a milestone review.

What about a new prototype?
Selected prototype builds start around $5k. They use the general inquiry path and are separate from the App Rescue Assessment, which requires existing software.
