# Organic content strategy

## Consolidation direction — September 27, 2026

The main commercial offer is custom software and business-platform development.
Mobile development and AI integration are focused service paths, not separate
identities for the business. Keep the homepage and services overview organized
around those three paths, with relevant existing project evidence beside them.

- Platform development and recovery: `/hire-web-app-development-company.html`
- Mobile development: `/senior-mobile-app-developer.html`
- AI integration and automation: `/ai-integration-consultant.html`

Specialist pages (including iOS, React Native, healthcare, and technical advisory)
remain available for distinct buyer needs. Small consulting packages are optional
starting points in the services-page disclosure, not the primary presentation of
the business. Process and commercial terms belong on the engagement and pricing
pages rather than repeated long sections throughout the site.

Do not create another keyword-variant service page by default. Review existing
content first. Before merging or retiring the overlapping AI or mobile pages,
check URL-level Search Console queries, traffic, links, and active ad destinations.
This pass preserves every existing URL, canonical, and noindex setting. Legacy
paid-only landing pages must not be enabled for indexing indiscriminately.

Evaluate future changes using non-branded commercial impressions and clicks by
service page, qualified project inquiries, and enquiry-to-engagement outcomes.
Cleaner presentation is a usability change, not evidence that rankings improved
or that the earlier traffic decline has been diagnosed.

## Publication gate

Before creating a new article, answer all six questions:

1. Which cluster owns it?
2. Is its search intent materially different from an existing article?
3. What first-hand observation, measurement, artifact, or implementation lesson makes it distinct?
4. Would updating a primary guide serve readers better than creating another URL?
5. Which primary guide and commercial service page will it link to?
6. What reader decision will be easier after reading it?

If questions 2–4 do not have strong answers, update an existing article instead of publishing a new one.
