## Left accent — software products

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original decorative homepage accent for a senior custom software development consultancy, not a blog cover.
Style/medium: refined photorealistic 3D product visualization with architectural precision; premium, restrained, credible, not cartoonish or clip art.
Scene/backdrop: deep navy blue studio background (#0a2540), subtle depth, cobalt and cyan edge lighting, brushed silver and dark glass materials.
Composition/framing: very wide landscape, approximately 2.2:1 aspect ratio. Compact large-scale central subject, simple unmistakable silhouette readable when displayed at only 176 by 80 pixels. Keep all important forms within the central 65% width and central 65% height so responsive cropping remains attractive. Fill the rectangular frame, no white margin, no border.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no people, no faces, no robots, no brains, no rockets, no stock-photo office scene, no article headline. This is conceptual software imagery, not a purported real client screenshot.
Primary request: custom software and native mobile product development, expressed as one elegant thin desktop application display and one upright smartphone beside it. Both screens show only broad cobalt and cyan interface shapes—few simple panels, a clean chart silhouette, and restrained geometric controls with no text. Close three-quarter product view, grounded soft reflections, clean believable device geometry. Devices should be the obvious visual focus; avoid miniature clutter.
```
