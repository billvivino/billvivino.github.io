Our Technology

Our Working Model

Bill Vivino Technology is engaged as an independent engineering company and technical partner—not as staff augmentation, a subordinate implementation resource, or personnel within a client’s organizational hierarchy.

We work directly with founders, CEOs, CTOs, architects, and internal engineering teams. Close technical coordination is encouraged, and we are happy to work with a client’s technical leadership. Our engagements do not create employee-style reporting relationships inside the client’s organization.

Our engagements generally follow one of two models:

Primary technical partner

We own the software-development function end to end, including architecture, technical planning, implementation strategy, delivery, and technical risk.

Owner of a defined technical domain

When a CTO or internal engineering organization owns the broader technical program, we take end-to-end ownership of clearly defined systems or initiatives, such as mobile architecture, cross-platform delivery, a core application, an integration, or a major product initiative.

Our strongest engagements give us clearly defined areas where we own technical decisions and outcomes from end to end. Depending on the client, that may include software architecture, implementation strategy, product engineering, mobile and web delivery, integrations, deployment, technical risk, or the complete software-development function.

We partner effectively with CTOs and internal engineering teams by taking full ownership of agreed systems or initiatives. The relationship remains a peer consulting relationship with clear responsibilities, authority, and accountability.

Clear engagement boundaries

Material decisions involving scope, hours, responsibilities, authority, commercial terms, and continuation of the engagement remain principal-to-principal between Bill Vivino Technology and the client.

If an organization intends to retain all architecture, engineering direction, staffing authority, and technical ownership internally while using Bill Vivino Technology solely to execute implementation tasks, that is a different service model and not one we offer on a long-term basis.

We believe responsibility, authority, and accountability should remain aligned. Clients receive the most value from Bill Vivino Technology when we are trusted to own the technical outcomes for which we are engaged.
