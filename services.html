A Clear Starting Point, with Room for Ongoing Delivery

We can act as your primary technical partner or own an agreed system alongside your CTO and engineers.

Start with the business outcome and what exists today. Agree the scope, access, responsibilities, spending cap, and review dates. Use demonstrations, test evidence, and documented findings to decide what comes next.

New builds and ongoing development are scoped in stages. If an existing product is stalled or unclear, a fixed-fee App Rescue Assessment is one way to establish the next step—not a requirement for every project.

Scope, budget, and continuation decisions remain between the client and Bill Vivino Technology. Long-term task-only staffing is outside this independent consulting model.
