Build, Stabilize, or Connect Your Platform

Launch a New Platform

Turn a defined business workflow into an application people can use. Plan the first release around its users, data, integrations, and operating requirements, then build and test it in reviewable stages.

Make an Existing System Usable

Start with the failures blocking users or delivery. Inspect the application and its dependencies, agree the priority fixes, and decide what to retain, repair, or rebuild. A troubled system does not automatically need a complete rewrite.

Connect the Systems Around It

Bring APIs, databases, reporting, and mobile clients into the same product plan. Address access rules, synchronization, failure handling, and ownership at the boundaries between systems.
