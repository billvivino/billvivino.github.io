Payment Structure

Fund an accepted proposal in one of two ways

Option 1

Discounted Prepaid Total

Pay the exact proposal fee before work begins and receive the dollar discount shown in your proposal. The approved PayPal payment link matches that proposal and project.

Option 2

Fixed Weekly Plan

Pay the standard proposal fee through a defined number of weekly installments. The proposal shows the payment amount, number of payments, total, and schedule before the first payment is due.

What the fixed price covers

Payment covers only the delivery phase and deliverables in the accepted written proposal. Added or changed work requires written approval and may change the fee or schedule.

Project-specific terms address assumptions, third-party costs, scheduling, missed payments, cancellation, and any unused prepaid balance before checkout is made available.
