Agree the finish line before the work starts

For a custom build, paid discovery defines the inputs, deliverables, acceptance checks, and delivery plan. You then receive a fixed build quote and decide whether to proceed. Builds include testing and source-code handoff; scope changes and third-party charges are separate.

Payment and scheduling

We agree the discovery fee and scope before discovery starts, then quote the build separately. The Working Prototype keeps its defined scope and 4–6 week schedule from kickoff, with no additional discovery fee. Each build proposal sets its price and payment schedule.

Availability, access, feedback requirements, cancellation, and any treatment of unused prepaid funds are agreed before payment.
